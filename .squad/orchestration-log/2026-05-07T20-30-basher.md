## Summary

Basher investigated and fixed the transfer-service account lookup failure. Two root causes were identified and resolved:

1. **docker-compose inter-service URLs missing port 8080** — .NET 9 containers default to port 8080, but service URLs omitted the port, causing connection refused errors.
2. **Ownership check blocking cross-user transfers** — The `GetAccountByNumber` endpoint was returning 403 Forbidden for accounts not owned by the requesting user, which broke transfers to accounts owned by other users.

Both issues were fixed; the fix maintains docker-compose compatibility as required.
