## Rulings Delivered

### 1. Approval Card Information Architecture
- **What:** Approval detail card display language and field ordering
- **Finding:** Engine vocabulary (rung, escalators, slot indices) was surfacing in user-facing copy
- **Ruling:** Rewrite copy to be banker-centric; use only what a signer needs to decide
- **Semantics:** Evidence findings, approval payload, dual control narrative, signing attestation all derived from the approval state, not the policy engine's internal model
- **Manifest:** Captured in `danny-approval-card-information-architecture.md` (preserved in full — long, actively built spec)
- **Implementation Status:** Linus completed copy rewrites and separation-of-duties verification; Turk added `ApprovalDisplay.cs` contract for display-only fields

### 2. Human Override & Counter-Proposal Model
- **What:** Policy ruling on how a banker overrides a proposal and counter-proposes an alternative
- **Options Presented:** 
  - Option A: New action form — banker supplies new action, payload, and facts; UI treats as fresh proposal
  - Option B: Override form — banker overrides agent recommendation; escalates to L2 + supervisor assessment
  - Combo (A + B): Support both paths
- **Ruling Chosen:** Brian chose option A + B (both paths supported)
- **Manifest:** Captured in `danny-human-override-counter-proposal.md` (preserved in full — long, foundational spec)
- **Implementation Status:** Turk implemented counter-proposal support via `context.supersedes`, churn guard, L2-floor escalator without `raiseBy`

- **Status:** Proposed; awaiting Danny's semantic ruling
