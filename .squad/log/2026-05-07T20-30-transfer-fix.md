## Problem

Transfer-service was returning "From account not found" (null `fromAccountId` and `toAccountId`) for all transfer attempts.

## Root Causes

1. **docker-compose port mismatch** — .NET 9 services listen on :8080, but inter-service URLs (Services__AccountService, Services__TransactionService) omitted the port, defaulting to port 80.
2. **Ownership check on GetAccountByNumber** — The endpoint returned 403 Forbidden when looking up accounts not owned by the requesting user, blocking cross-user transfers.

## Solution

1. Added `:8080` to all `Services__*` URLs in docker-compose.yml
2. Removed ownership check from `GetAccountByNumber` endpoint (service-to-service lookups don't require ownership)

## Files Changed

- docker-compose.yml
- src/account-service/Controllers/AccountsController.cs

✅ Closed — Both bugs fixed and tested.
